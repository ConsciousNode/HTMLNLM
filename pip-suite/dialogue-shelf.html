Dialogue Shelf: help

Compare several conversations exported from Multi-Pip Chat (⤓ Export) and see how alike or different they turned out. Nothing leaves this device.

How to use it

  1. Drop two or more export files on the shelf, or tap to pick them.
  2. Read the comparison.
  3. ⤓ Report downloads it. Clear empties the shelf.

What it measures

  • Distinct-1 / 2 / 3: how varied the wording is. It's the share of single words, word pairs and word triples that are unique. Higher means less repetitive.
  • Pairwise overlap: how much two conversations share, phrase for phrase.
  • Replayed openings: runs that started word for word the same (the same seed and settings do that). It also notes when the replay length is the context window + 1: the chats stay identical until the window first has to drop a line.
  • Recurring phrases: four-word phrases that keep coming back, after any replayed opening.
  • Invented names: names the models made up that recur across runs.
  • Flags: each run's filter flags (speaker slips, garble, repeats, heavy themes and so on). These only point things out; nothing is changed.

Good for checking whether a batch of synthetic dialogue is genuinely varied, or for comparing settings (stateful vs. not, different seeds or temperatures).
